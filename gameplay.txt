PART 1: THE VISUAL EXPERIENCE (UI/UX)
The vibe must be Underground Poker meets Street Fighter. Dark mode. High contrast. Serious.

1. The Logo
Concept: "The Spark."

Visual: Two speech bubbles colliding. One is jagged (Red/Attack), one is a smooth shield (Blue/Defense).

Vibe: Conflict and Control.

2. The Lobby (Searching Screen)
Visual: Dark screen. A radar/sonar pulse in the center.

Text: "Scanning Local Network..." -> "Frame Detected." -> "Opponent Found."

The Trick: Show the Opponent's "Card" before the match starts.

Avatar: (e.g., A pixelated skull).

Name: Frame_Killa.

Rank: "Silver III".

Win Rate: "64%".

Why: Seeing their stats makes them feel real.

3. The Arena (The Match)
Layout: Split Screen.

Top Half: The Opponent (Avatar + Health Bar named "STATUS").

Bottom Half: You (Avatar + Health Bar).

The Center: The Scenario Text appears here.

The Timer: A thin line burning down rapidly (Red).

Animation: When you take damage, your screen shakes slightly. When you win a round, your avatar glows Blue.

4. Post-Match
Visual: "VICTORY" or "FRAMED" (Loss).

Progression: Show a bar filling up towards the next Rank.

Rivalry: A button that says "Rematch Request Sent..." (The bot can randomly accept or decline).
